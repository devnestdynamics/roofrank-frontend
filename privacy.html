Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026
Draft. This is a starting point — RoofRank's privacy policy will be reviewed by counsel before launch. The principles below describe our intended practices.

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect the email address you sign up with and a password (stored as a one-way hash). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile photo from Google but no other account data.

As you use RoofRank, we store the deals you save, watchlist items, reports you generate, and any notes or scenario adjustments you make. We also log basic usage events (which pages you visit, which deals you view) to understand product performance.

What we don't collect

  • Your name, address, or contact info beyond email — unless you opt in to SMS alerts (Pro).
  • Payment card details. Stripe handles all billing; we only see a subscription status.
  • Any data from third parties about you (no purchased lists, no enriched profiles).

How we use your data

  • To run the product — show you deals, save your watchlist, send you alerts you've opted into.
  • To improve scoring and AI quality (in aggregate, not tied to your identity).
  • To bill you if you're on a paid plan.
  • To respond when you contact support.

Who we share with

Only the service providers that make RoofRank work, each operating under their own privacy policies:

  • Stripe — billing
  • Amazon Web Services — hosting
  • Anthropic — AI analysis (we send deal data, not your account identity)
  • Resend — transactional email
  • AWS End User Messaging (Amazon SNS) — SMS alerts (if you opt in)
  • RentCast, ATTOM, HUD — these are data sources, not recipients. We pull listings + market data from them; they don't see anything about you.

We don't sell your data. We don't share it with advertisers. We don't run ads on RoofRank.

SMS opt-in data — never shared with third parties

If you opt in to RoofRank SMS notifications, the phone number and SMS opt-in information you provide is used solely to deliver the SMS messages you have explicitly opted into (sign-in codes, Strong Buy alerts, price-drop notifications). We do not share, sell, rent, lease, or otherwise disclose your mobile opt-in data, phone number, or SMS consent records to third parties, affiliates, marketing partners, or data brokers for any purpose. The only exception is the SMS service provider (AWS End User Messaging / Amazon SNS) that we use to physically deliver the messages, governed by their own privacy practices, and only for the limited purpose of routing the message to your carrier.

You can revoke SMS consent at any time by replying STOP to any RoofRank message or by disabling SMS alerts in your account settings.

Cookies

RoofRank uses minimal cookies — primarily a session token to keep you logged in, and a CSRF token on form submissions. We use localStorage to cache your watchlist and chat history client-side so the app loads fast.

If we add product analytics (e.g., PostHog) before launch, we'll update this section before turning it on.

Your rights

You can delete your account at any time from settings — this removes your saved deals, notes, and account record. Email privacy@roofrank.io if you want to export your data or have questions.

Children

RoofRank is for adults evaluating real estate investments. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change anything material, we'll notify you by email before the change takes effect.
