Pros / cons of staying
Pros: No domain/legal cost. SEO equity preserved. Recognizable already. Name describes what the product does — easier to explain.

Cons: "RoofRank" reads as descriptive (we rank roofs), not as a brand. Caps you somewhat — if you ever broaden to commercial or non-residential, "roof" becomes limiting. The mark, even refined, isn't as ownable as a brand-name mark could be.
Option B
Deckersnew brand
Logo Lockup
Deckers Deckers Deckers
Three stacked floors of a multifamily building. The top "floor" is the bright accent — the deal worth your time today. Mark is more distinctive than RoofRank's because the silhouette is unique (vs three vertical bars, which are common). Also extends naturally to score viz.
Voice + Tagline
"Every multi-deck building, examined."
"Deckers" is multifamily-native vocabulary — it tells you the audience without explaining. Voice can be more familiar/insider ("the deckers in your market"), less formal than RoofRank.
