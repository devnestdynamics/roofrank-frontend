What to do with this: these are all rendered in HTML so they're responsive mockups, not the final images you'd upload to Meta. To turn one into an actual ad creative:

1. Pick a variant (my recommendation: B for first paid test — quote-card creatives outperform pure data-density ones for cold audiences)
2. Recreate the creative panel in Canva at 1080 × 1080 (square, works for FB + IG feed + IG carousel)
3. Also export at 1080 × 1920 (vertical) for IG Stories + Reels placements
4. Upload to Meta Ads Manager · paste the primary text + headline + description from the previous Claude message

One-shot Canva trick: screenshot the creative panel from this mockup in your browser at 2x zoom → import directly to Canva → tweak. Total time: 10 min per variant.
