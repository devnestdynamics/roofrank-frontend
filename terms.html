Not financial advice

RoofRank is not investment advice, tax advice, or legal advice. We are not a licensed real estate broker, financial advisor, or fiduciary. The scores, narratives, and projections shown by RoofRank are estimates based on publicly-available data and standard financial formulas.

Before making any real estate purchase, you should:

  • Independently verify all property data with the seller, listing agent, and your own inspections.
  • Confirm financial projections with your accountant or CPA — especially anything tax-related.
  • Review the property's title, condition, zoning, and rental history with a licensed agent and attorney.
  • Talk to a lender about actual financing terms — our defaults are illustrative, not quotes.
