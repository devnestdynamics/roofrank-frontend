Changelog

What's shipped on RoofRank. We update this page as features roll out. Grouped by week, most recent first.

Week of June 2 · 2026
Self-serve Pro checkout · welcome-to-Pro experience · launch-readiness sweep
  • Shipped
    Welcome-to-Pro experience. A green banner now greets new Pro subscribers on the dashboard with a recap of what's unlocked. A confirmation email arrives in parallel.
  • Shipped
    Re-written verification email. Subject changed from "Verify your RoofRank email" to "Welcome to RoofRank — one click to start." Body leads with what RoofRank is, not the verify mechanics.
  • Fixed
    Self-serve Pro upgrade was completely broken. The pricing-page Start Pro CTA didn't fire Stripe Checkout, the backend success URL pointed at a non-existent page, and the checkout endpoint required a raw Stripe price ID the frontend couldn't know. Full end-to-end flow now works.
  • Fixed
    Anonymous → Pro signup flow. If you click Start Pro while logged out, sign up, then land on the dashboard, you're now auto-routed to Stripe Checkout instead of dropped into the free dashboard.
  • Fixed
    Manual analyzer accepted 5-6 unit properties the rest of the product couldn't score correctly. Tightened to 2-4 unit as scoped.
  • Fixed
    Hardened the SMS inbound webhook. The endpoint now verifies AWS SNS signatures before processing STOP/HELP keywords. Without this, anyone could have spoofed an opt-out for someone else's number.
  • Changed
    Tightened CORS in production. Only the canonical RoofRank origin is allowed now; localhost and missing-origin requests are rejected.
  • Changed
    Added timeouts on external API calls (ATTOM, Anthropic). Slow upstreams can no longer cascade into a slow RoofRank.
  • Changed
    Wired Sentry error capture into all background jobs and fire-and-forget paths. Means we see real signal on the things that used to fail silently.
Week of May 26 · 2026
Manual analyzer rebuild · pricing pivot · SMS plumbing
  • Shipped
    Manual analyzer rebuild. ATTOM-only now — drop a US address and we'll pull units, year built, taxes, and score the deal. Save it to My Deals with one tap, delete with a two-tap confirm.
  • Shipped
    SMS opt-in flow with double opt-in. Settings page now collects your phone, sends a 6-digit verify code, and only enables alerts after you confirm. STOP/HELP keywords honored carrier-side and ours.
  • Shipped
    Enterprise tier replaces Team self-serve. Pro stays at $49/mo with unlimited manual analysis. Larger teams and brokerages contact us directly.
  • Shipped
    Dashboard mobile polish. Cashflow now stays visible on small screens (was disappearing under 480px). Manual + Add a Property CTA shows a Pro badge for free users.
  • Changed
    Landing positioning refresh. Lead with the math — live mortgage rates, real rents, actual property tax — and push alerts the moment a deal cashflows. We no longer claim to score "every" listing.
Earlier this spring
Migrated to Cloudflare Pages · onboarding redesign · AI deal narrative
  • Changed
    Frontend moved from Netlify to Cloudflare Pages. Faster, fewer cold starts, and no surprise bandwidth bills.
  • Shipped
    Onboarding redesign. Pick your markets, see live deal counts, get a real preview before you commit to anything.
  • Shipped
    AI deal narrative on every deal. Bull case, bear case, what the math actually says — generated per-deal, cached, signed by the Analyst.
  • Shipped
    Ask the Analyst chat. On every deal detail page, ask follow-ups about the numbers, the neighborhood, or the offer strategy.
  • Shipped
    Condition lens. Toggle as-is, updated, or renovated on any deal — rents and cashflow recompute live (Pro).
