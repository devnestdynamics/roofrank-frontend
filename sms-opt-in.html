RoofRank SMS Opt-in Workflow

This page describes the complete opt-in workflow for RoofRank SMS notifications, the messages you may receive, and how to stop or get help. RoofRank is operated by DevNest Dynamics LLC (dba RoofRank), based in Massachusetts.

How to opt in

RoofRank uses a double opt-in process. You must explicitly enable SMS in your account AND verify your phone number with a one-time code before any alerts are sent.

1
Create or sign in to your RoofRank account

Visit roofrank.io and create an account (or sign in).

2
Open the Settings page

Navigate to roofrank.io/roofrank-settings.html (also reachable via the avatar menu in the top right of the dashboard).

3
Add your US mobile phone number

In the "Phone & SMS alerts" section, enter your US mobile number and click "Save phone." This stores the number but does not enable SMS alerts yet — verification is required.

4
Request a verification code

Click "Send verification code." RoofRank sends a single SMS containing a 6-digit code to the phone number you entered.

From RoofRank
RoofRank: Your sign-in code is 482591. Don't share this code. Reply STOP to unsubscribe.
5
Enter the code to confirm opt-in

Enter the 6-digit code back in the Settings page. On success, RoofRank flips your sms_opt_in status to true, records the consent timestamp, and you'll begin receiving alerts on the schedule below.

From RoofRank
RoofRank: You're opted in to RoofRank Alerts. Receive up to 20 msgs/month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel.

Messages you may receive

Once opted in, RoofRank may send you the following message types — all from the same phone number, branded "RoofRank:":

  • Sign-in verification codes when you request passwordless authentication via SMS (transactional, on-demand).
  • "Strong Buy" alerts when newly-listed 2-4 unit multifamily properties in your saved Massachusetts markets meet our cashflow threshold.
  • Price-drop notifications on properties you've added to your watchlist.

Message frequency

Recurring. You may receive up to approximately 20 messages per month from RoofRank, depending on activity in your saved markets and watchlist. Frequency varies — there are no guaranteed messages.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. RoofRank does not charge a fee for SMS messages, but your wireless carrier's standard message and data rates apply per your mobile plan.

How to stop messages — reply STOP

Reply STOP (or any of: UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT) to any RoofRank message. You will be unsubscribed immediately and permanently from all RoofRank SMS notifications, and you'll receive one final confirmation message. You can also disable SMS alerts at any time in your account settings.

How to get help — reply HELP

Reply HELP (or INFO) to any RoofRank message for support contact information. You can also email support@roofrank.io for help with your account or the SMS program.

Privacy and data handling

Phone numbers and SMS opt-in information you provide to RoofRank are used solely to deliver the messages you've opted into. We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose your mobile opt-in data, phone number, or SMS consent records to third parties, affiliates, or marketing partners for any purpose.
