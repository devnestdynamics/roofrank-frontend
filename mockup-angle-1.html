Angle 1 — Analyst-led positioning

Reposition RoofRank from "deal screener with AI features" to "an AI analyst that scores every multifamily deal in your market." Same product, sharper story. Three surfaces shift to make the brand match what's actually built: landing hero, deal-detail (chat moves up), and dashboard nav (persistent Ask-the-Analyst entry). No engineering pivot — copy + UX reorder + ~3-5 days for the deal-agnostic chat surface.

Surface 1 — Landing page hero

Current hero leads with a benefit ("Know if a multifamily cashflows in 30 seconds"). Proposed leads with WHO is doing the analysis. Receipt also shifts from "look at this fast result" to "hear from the analyst" — the analyst's voice becomes the proof, not the speed.

Proposed: a persistent "Ask the Analyst" entry at the nav level opens a deal-agnostic chat. ~3-5 days of work — chat infra already exists, just needs a market-scope prompt path.
