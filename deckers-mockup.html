How this works:
  • Every deal card carries the full Deckers silhouette — roof + three floors — next to its score number. The silhouette is always there, on every card, in every tier.
  • Color is the signal. Strong Buy is forest. Buy is forest-3. Watch is amber. Pass is rose. The number AND the silhouette share the same color so the eye reads them as one unit.
  • Shape never changes — even a Pass deal carries the brand mark. We're not hiding floors or leaving the tile empty. The deal is in the system; its color tells you the verdict.
  • The canonical brand mark in the app bar stays sage on forest — that's the brand identity, separate from any deal's score. So the silhouette serves double duty: brand mark when it's sage, score viz when it's tier-colored.
  • Roof: stroke currentColor, 2.4px. Floors: fill currentColor, three thin bars. Same SVG path renders every state — only the parent's color class changes.
