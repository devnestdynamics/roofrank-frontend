The Scoring Engine

8 metrics. One score.
Complete clarity.

Every metric a seasoned investor looks at — calculated, weighted, and combined into a single score so you spend 30 seconds on a deal instead of 3 hours.

01
Cash-on-Cash Return
Annual pre-tax cash flow ÷ total cash invested. The number that tells you how hard your money is working.
Target ≥ 8%
02
Cap Rate
NOI ÷ purchase price. Measures yield independent of financing — essential for comparing across markets.
Target ≥ 6.5%
03
DSCR
Debt Service Coverage Ratio. NOI ÷ annual debt payments. Lenders require ≥ 1.25 — we surface this before you waste time on financing.
Target ≥ 1.25
04
Gross Rent Multiplier
Purchase price ÷ annual gross rent. A quick check for overpaying. A GRM above 12× usually doesn't pencil out.
Target ≤ 10×
05
Price per Unit
Total acquisition cost ÷ number of units. Calibrated to New England market realities — not national averages that don't apply here.
Market-calibrated
06
Net Operating Income
Effective gross income minus all operating expenses. The foundation of every professional valuation — we show you the real number, not an optimistic proforma.
Higher is better
07
CapEx Reserve Score
Based on year built, roofing cycles, HVAC age, and expected major repairs over a 10-year hold. Old buildings aren't automatically bad — they just need to pencil with reserves.
Age-adjusted
08
Neighborhood Grade
Walk score, school quality, crime index, and rent growth trajectory by ZIP. Tells you the story behind the numbers — is this location getting better or worse?
A–D rating
