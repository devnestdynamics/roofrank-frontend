Common questions
What counts as a "report"? ▾
A report is a full financial analysis run in the Analyzer — where you enter your own numbers (rents, expenses, down payment) and get a full proforma with score. Browsing the deal feed and viewing pre-computed scores doesn't count toward your limit.
What markets are covered? ▾
Currently 9 New England markets: Boston MA, Providence RI, Hartford CT, Worcester MA, Manchester NH, New Haven CT, Springfield MA, Bridgeport CT, and Lowell MA. National expansion is on the roadmap — join the waitlist if you want a specific market.
How accurate are the scores? ▾
Scores are computed from public listing data, tax records, and RentCast rent estimates. They're a reliable first-pass filter — not a substitute for your own proforma. Use the Analyzer to pressure-test any deal before making an offer.
Can I cancel anytime? ▾
Yes. Cancel from your billing page at any time — no questions, no penalties. You'll keep access through the end of your current billing period.
Is this investment advice? ▾
No. RoofRank is a deal-scoring tool, not a financial advisor. Scores and signals are based on financial metrics — always do your own due diligence, consult a licensed advisor, and verify numbers independently before purchasing any property.
