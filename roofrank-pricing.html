Common questions
What markets are included? ▾
Currently live in 5 Massachusetts cities: Lynn, Worcester, Salem, Revere, and Framingham — all with real listings and nightly scoring. We're expanding across New England next; Pro users get every new market the day it launches.
How accurate are the scores? ▾
Scores are based on PITI cash flow (mortgage + taxes + insurance), HUD Fair Market Rents, and real tax data from ATTOM, recomputed nightly at today's Freddie Mac rate. They're a starting point — you verify with your CPA, lender, and agent before you offer.
How often is data updated? ▾
Listings are scored every night at 2am ET. New listings appear in your feed the morning after they hit the market.
What AI features are included with Pro? ▾
Pro includes AI deal narratives (a written bull and bear case on every Strong Buy / Buy listing), Ask the Analyst (an AI chat on any deal that knows the property's numbers + your local market), and the AI Market Brief email when there's something worth your time. All powered by Claude Sonnet 4.6.
Can I cancel anytime? ▾
Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Click "Manage subscription" in the dashboard menu to cancel directly from Stripe — you retain access until the end of your current billing period.
