Common questions
What markets are included? ▾
Currently live: Lynn, MA and Worcester, MA with real data. Boston, Providence, Hartford, New Haven, Manchester, Springfield, Bridgeport and Lowell are coming soon. Pro users get all markets as they launch.
How accurate are the scores? ▾
Scores are based on PITI cash flow (mortgage + taxes + insurance), HUD Fair Market Rents, and real tax data from ATTOM. They're a starting point — you verify before you offer.
How often is data updated? ▾
Listings are scored every night at 2am. New listings appear in your feed the morning after they hit the market.
When will AI features be available? ▾
AI deal narratives and Ask the Analyst are in active development. Pro subscribers get early access when they launch.
Can I cancel anytime? ▾
Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel from your account settings and you retain access until end of billing period.
